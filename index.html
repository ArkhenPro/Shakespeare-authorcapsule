Who Was William Shakespeare??

William Shakespeare was a super famous English guy who wrote heaps of plays, poems, and acted in London. He was born in a place called Stratford-upon-Avon in 1564 (we don't know the exact day but he was baptized on April 26). He died on April 23, 1616. He is basically the most important writer ever in the English language!

He lived during the Elizabethan era which means Queen Elizabeth I was the boss. His dad made gloves and his family wasn't super rich but they weren't poor either.

Fast Facts / Author Capsule Specs

  • Nickname: The Bard of Avon 👑
  • Total Plays Written: Like 37 or 39 plays (depends on who you ask because he collabed with other guys)
  • Sonnets: 154 poems! (That is a lot of rhyming)
  • His Theater: The Globe Theatre in London (it burned down once because a fake cannon hit the roof but they rebuilt it)
  • Wife: Anne Hathaway (Not the actress from the movies, a different one)
  • Weird Will Fact: When he died he only left his wife his "second-best bed". Pretty mean lol.

His Most Famous Stuff

He wrote three types of plays: Comedies (funny), Tragedies (everyone dies at the end), and Histories (about real kings). Here are the ones you probably have to read in high school:

Play Title Type What happens? (Spoiler Alert)
Romeo and Juliet Tragedy Two teenagers fall in love but their families hate each other so it ends really badly.
Hamlet Tragedy A ghost tells a prince to get revenge on his uncle. Hamlet talks to a skull.
Macbeth Tragedy Witches tell a guy he will be king so he becomes evil and listens to his wife too much.
A Midsummer Night's Dream Comedy Fairies run around a forest putting love juice in peoples eyes. One guy turns into a donkey.

Words He Actually Invented!

If you think Shakespeare's English is annoying, he actually invented over 1,000 words we still use today. Without him we wouldn't have these words:

👉 Eyeball 👉 Bedroom 👉 Gossip 👉 Swagger 👉 Zany

Imagine not being able to say bedroom!!
